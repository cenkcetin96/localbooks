LocalBooks puts invoices, receipts, expenses and accountant-ready records on your MacBook. Built for Thai and expat SME owners and managers. Free, open source and MIT licensed.

[**Download LocalBooks for Mac — no Terminal needed**](https://github.com/cenkcetin96/localbooks/releases/download/v1.2.0/LocalBooks-1.2.0-macOS-AppleSilicon.dmg)

### What changed in 1.2.0

- Keep a chart of accounts for each company. Open the **Accountant** tab on invoices, receipts, expenses, and bank transactions to review automatic debit and credit suggestions or save a balanced manual entry.
- Calculate account movements and balances by date and currency, then export them as CSV.
- Review Thai reporting mappings, group saved THB movements by DBD category, and include P.N.D.2 in withholding worksheets when applicable.
- Export accounting entries, the chart of accounts, Thai reporting mappings, and DBD movements in the company’s accountant pack.
- Find receipts by company code and payment month, with numbers such as `DEMO-RE-2026-11-00001`. The suggested full payment adjusts for withholding until you edit the payment amount.

Existing records and previously issued PDFs remain in place. Download a backup, quit LocalBooks, then replace the app in Applications and reopen it.

### Download and install — no Terminal needed

1. Download **LocalBooks-1.2.0-macOS-AppleSilicon.dmg** below. Requires an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) running **macOS 15 Sequoia or later**. Check **Apple menu → About This Mac**.
2. Open the downloaded file and drag **LocalBooks** onto **Applications**.
3. Open **Applications → LocalBooks**. If macOS blocks it, choose **Done**, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**, and confirm **Open**. This community build is not Apple notarized. See [Apple’s instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445).
4. Add your company and customer, then create your first invoice. The [short onboarding tour](https://github.com/cenkcetin96/localbooks#five-minute-onboarding-tour) walks you through the rest.

Python, the PDF engine, PDF tools and English/Thai OCR are included. No Homebrew, Chrome installation, subscription or cloud account is needed. Intel Macs are not supported by this download.

Your records stay on your Mac. The download includes no real business records. Existing LocalBooks records are reused from your Mac’s Application Support folder; download a backup before updating.

### Prefer to make it your own?

Use LocalBooks as-is, or fork the source and customize it with Codex, Claude Code, Cursor or another AI coding tool. The [developer guide](https://github.com/cenkcetin96/localbooks/blob/main/DEVELOPMENT.md) starts with a separate demo profile so you can experiment away from your live books.

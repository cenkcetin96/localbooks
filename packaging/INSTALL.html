Your business paperwork, on your Mac.

LocalBooks is a free app for Thai and expat SME owners and managers. No Terminal, subscription or cloud account needed.

  1. Drag LocalBooks onto Applications in this disk image.
  2. Open Applications → LocalBooks.
  3. If macOS blocks the app, choose Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway and confirm Open. This community build is not Apple notarized. Apple’s instructions.
  4. Add your company and a customer. Create a draft invoice, review it, and issue your first PDF.

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) with macOS 15 or later. Download a backup before updating an existing installation.

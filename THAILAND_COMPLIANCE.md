# Preparing Thai business records with LocalBooks

Keep each legal company in its own profile. Select the company before adding invoices, expenses, bank records or compliance evidence. The accountant export is scoped to the selected company and accounting year; a full database backup contains every company.

## Set up your company

In **My companies**, enter your legal details and actual VAT-registration status. In **Compliance → Company settings**, confirm the first preparation year, employee count, financial year-end and accountant. New profiles use the current year, zero employees and the saved company VAT setting. These defaults do not establish historical tax or payroll obligations.

Leave year-end unconfirmed until it has been checked. Changing the accounting period can flag saved work for review; it does not move prior worksheets automatically.

## Monthly document routine

1. Save sales invoices and payment receipts under the issuing company.
2. Upload supplier documents and review extracted fields against the originals.
3. Import and review statements, link existing payments and check missing supporting documents.
4. Attach customer withholding certificates to the relevant payments.
5. Ask your accountant to confirm applicable filings, calculations and actual due dates. Save acceptance evidence and payment receipts separately.

## Accountant handoff

Use **Export accountant pack** for the company and accounting year. The ZIP includes source records, worksheets, statuses, missing-document requests, available PDFs/originals and a hash manifest. It is a review package, not a submitted return, official filing import or audited accounts.

VAT, withholding and corporate tax worksheets require explicit reviewed inputs. Current company settings do not prove previous registration or payroll history. Filing tasks start Unknown. Baseline dates in the app need verification against current official guidance, holiday rules and any applicable extensions. Consult the [Revenue Department](https://www.rd.go.th) and [Department of Business Development](https://www.dbd.go.th), together with your accountant.

LocalBooks does not submit returns, calculate payroll, create a double-entry general ledger or certify legal compliance. Missing-document checks cover saved records and cannot establish that the books are complete.
